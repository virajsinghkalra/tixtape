# Gig sources: research for indie, club and acoustic shows in India

_Gig Coverage team · 2026-11-21. I fetched each robots.txt and terms page myself on this date, with `curl` and a non-personal User-Agent. Where a terms page couldn't be read, the table says so and says why._

## Why we missed "Sketchbook by Bhuwin Khursija"

- **The event:** Mumbai, 5, 6 and 9 Dec 2026, 7:30 PM, organised by OML.
- **Where its facts live today:**
  - BookMyShow, the ticketing page;
  - Instagram posts by the artist and OML;
  - the artist's Linktree.
- **No allowed source has it.** I searched with Tavily and found nothing on a news outlet, a venue site, the organiser's site (oml.in sits behind a WAF and has no event listing) or any open listing. All three places above forbid automated collection (see the table).
- **What a polite collector can do:** learn that the show probably exists, from a search result's title, and hand the link to a human. It can't confirm or publish the show on its own. D-073 builds exactly that: a "lead" for the Lead's review, plus automatic confirmation whenever an allowed official page exists.

## Rule: crawling versus reading a page a user gave us

| Case                                                               | What it is                                                                        | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Crawling** (scheduled collector, artist-first discovery)         | We decide which URLs to fetch, repeatedly, at scale                               | robots.txt **and** the terms must allow it. PoliteFetcher enforces robots.txt, 1 req/s per host, the SSRF guard and byte caps. Denied domains are never fetched.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **A single page a user explicitly gave us** (a pasted link, later) | One fetch, triggered by a person, of one public URL, the way a link preview works | robots.txt is written for crawlers (RFC 9309), and user-triggered fetchers such as link previews generally aren't crawlers. **The site's terms still apply.** If the terms forbid any automated access (Linktree: "as a user, a Profile Visitor or in any other capacity"; BookMyShow; District), we don't fetch it even once. We store the link as a lead for a human. |
| **Search-engine results** (Tavily)                                 | A third party's index: URL, title and a snippet                                   | We may use a result's **URL and title** to decide where to look and to raise a lead. We never use a denied site's text, whether the snippet or Tavily's `raw_content`, as evidence or as stored facts. That would launder a scrape through a third party.                                                                                                               |

## Dry-run findings (2026-11-21)

- **Artist official sites work.** Peter Cat Recording Co.'s `pcrc.in/tour` (Wikidata P856, robots `Allow: /`) gave Bengaluru 9 Jan, Delhi NCR 31 Jan and Mumbai 19 Feb 2027. All three were checked by hand against the page. Guwahati, 10 Jan 2027, was skipped because Guwahati isn't in the city list.
- **Shows on ticketing sites become leads:** Seedhe Maut, Navi Mumbai, 13 Dec 2026 (Bandsintown); Travis Scott, Mumbai (BookMyShow); Anuv Jain, Delhi (trip.com). Each is a link for a human to check.
- **Bhuwin's Sketchbook:** search finds the BookMyShow page only with an exact query ("Bhuwin Sketchbook Mumbai December 2026"). The scheduled artist query finds his BookMyShow and Shazam pages for other shows. No allowed page has the Sketchbook dates.

## What this means for coverage

1. **Coverage now:** official artist sites plus allowed outlets, found by artist-first search; HighApe for club gigs; news outlets (existing).
2. **Indie shows sold only on BookMyShow, District or Skillboxes** still can't be confirmed automatically. The system now **finds** them (search-result leads) and puts them in front of the Lead with the link, instead of missing them silently.
3. **Ways to close the gap at $0, needing the user or Lead:**
   - ask Skillboxes and The Piano Man for a feed, or permission;
   - re-read the District terms in a real browser;
   - ask OML and other agencies for an RSS or ICS feed of their shows;
   - let users paste a BookMyShow or Linktree link (the Lead confirms it).
