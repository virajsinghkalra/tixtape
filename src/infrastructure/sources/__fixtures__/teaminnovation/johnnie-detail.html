Charlie Puth – Johnnie & Co. Music Festival

Music Festival | Pop | Live

Sat, 13th Feb, 2027

Mahalaxmi Racecourse, Mumbai

Experience Charlie Puth – Johnnie & Co. Music Festival

Get ready to witness global pop sensation Charlie Puth make his much-awaited India debut at the Johnnie & Co. Music Festival!

Known for his chart-topping hits, soulful vocals and exceptional musical brilliance, Charlie Puth has captivated audiences worldwide with iconic tracks like Attention, We Don’t Talk Anymore, See You Again, Left and Right and How Long.

Joining him on this spectacular musical experience is global pop star Ellie Goulding, alongside an exciting lineup featuring GINI, KAYAN, MALI and Taba Chake.

Experience an unforgettable celebration of music, energy and world-class performances on 13th February 2027 at Mahalaxmi Racecourse, Mumbai.

✨ Mark your calendars and get ready for a night to remember!
